Government of Nova-Berlandia

Learn about the constitutional institutions and leadership of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

The Emperor

Emperor Reginald II

The Emperor of Nova-Berlandia, otherwise known as the Imperial Berlandian Crown, is the constitutional head of state of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

The office in its present form was established on 20 August 2026, with Reginald II serving as the first monarch and incumbent Emperor.

The Emperor serves as a person of national unity and security, protects and upholds the Constitution, and represents Nova-Berlandia in diplomatic matters.

Head of State The constitutional head of the Empire.
National Security Represents the security and stability of the nation.
Protector of the Constitution Upholds and protects the Constitution.
Chief Diplomat Represents Nova-Berlandia in diplomatic affairs.
National Unity Serves as a symbol of unity for the Berlandian nation.
Parliament Serves as a lifelong sitting member of Parliament and may serve as Acting Speaker.

Personal Coat of Arms

Personal Coat of Arms of Emperor Reginald II

The personal coat of arms of Emperor Reginald II represents the Emperor and his position within the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

The arms are associated with the Emperor personally and are distinct from the national symbols of the Empire.

Constitutional Institutions

Parliament

Parliament is the legislative institution of Nova-Berlandia. The Emperor is a lifelong sitting member and may serve as Acting Speaker.

Government

The Government of Nova-Berlandia is responsible for the administration of the Empire and the implementation of government policy.

The Imperial Family

House of Torres

The Imperial Family is officially known as the House of Torres, consisting of the family of the incumbent Emperor.

The current heir apparent is Dayvian, Duke of Berchester.

Historical Background

The previous office of King of Nova-Berlandia existed from 28 November 2025 until 17 February 2026, with Shane serving as its only reigning monarch.

Following the dissolution of the United Commonwealth on 17 February 2026 and its replacement by the Republic of Keystone, the position of King was abolished.

On 20 August 2026, following the formation of the Provisional Government, the monarchy was restored. Shane was initially announced as King before the title was changed to Emperor of Nova-Berlandia.

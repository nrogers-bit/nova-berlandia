History of Nova-Berlandia

Explore the history of Nova-Berlandia and the development of its monarchy, government and constitutional system.

Overview

The history of Nova-Berlandia has included several changes in its political and constitutional structure. The modern Empire was established in August 2026 following the formation of a Provisional Government and the creation of the Imperial office.

Historical Timeline

28 November 2025

Kingdom of Nova-Berlandia

The office of King of Nova-Berlandia was established. Shane became the sole reigning monarch during this period.

17 February 2026

Dissolution of the United Commonwealth

The United Commonwealth was dissolved and replaced by the Republic of Keystone. The office of King of Nova-Berlandia was subsequently abolished.

20 August 2026

Formation of the Provisional Government

A Provisional Government was formed, beginning a new constitutional period for Nova-Berlandia.

20 August 2026

Establishment of the Imperial Office

Shane was initially announced as King before the title was changed to Emperor of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia. The Imperial office was established in its present form.

20 August 2026

Emperor Reginald II

Reginald II became the first Emperor of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia and the incumbent holder of the Imperial office.

The Modern Empire

The Imperial Constitution

Under the modern constitutional arrangement, the Emperor serves as the constitutional head of state and a person of national unity. The Emperor also protects and upholds the Constitution and serves as Chief Diplomat for the Berlandian nation.

The House of Torres

The Imperial Family is officially known as the House of Torres. The current heir apparent is Dayvian, Duke of Berchester.

Parliament and Government

The modern system provides for a Parliament and a Government appointed by the Emperor upon the recommendation of Parliament. The Emperor is also a lifelong sitting member of Parliament and may serve as Acting Speaker.

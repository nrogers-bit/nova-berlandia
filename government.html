Government of Nova-Berlandia

Learn about the constitutional structure of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia, the Imperial Crown and the institutions of government.

The Emperor

Emperor Reginald II

The Emperor of Nova-Berlandia, otherwise known as the Imperial Berlandian Crown, is the constitutional head of state of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

The office in its present form was established on 20 August 2026, with Reginald II serving as the first monarch and incumbent Emperor.

Head of State

The Emperor serves as the constitutional head of state of Nova-Berlandia.

National Unity

The Emperor serves as a person of national unity for the Berlandian nation.

Protector of the Constitution

The Emperor protects and upholds the Constitution of Nova-Berlandia.

Chief Diplomat

The Emperor serves as Chief Diplomat for the Berlandian nation.

Parliament

The Emperor is a lifelong sitting member of Parliament and may serve as Acting Speaker.

Formation of Government

The Emperor appoints the Government of Nova-Berlandia upon the recommendation of Parliament.

Personal Coat of Arms

Coat of Arms of Emperor Reginald II

The personal coat of arms of Emperor Reginald II represents the Emperor and the Imperial Crown of Nova-Berlandia.

Constitutional Institutions

Parliament

Parliament is the legislative institution of Nova-Berlandia. The Emperor is a lifelong sitting member of Parliament and may serve as Acting Speaker.

Government

The Government of Nova-Berlandia is appointed by the Emperor upon the recommendation of Parliament.

The Imperial Family

House of Torres

The Imperial Family of Nova-Berlandia is officially known as the House of Torres and consists of the family of the incumbent Emperor.

Heir Apparent: Dayvian, Duke of Berchester

Historical Background

The previous office of King of Nova-Berlandia existed from 28 November 2025 until 17 February 2026, with Shane serving as the sole reigning monarch.

On 17 February 2026, the United Commonwealth was dissolved and replaced by the Republic of Keystone, resulting in the abolition of the King of Nova-Berlandia.

Following the formation of the Provisional Government on 20 August 2026, Shane was initially announced as King before the title was changed to Emperor of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

The present Imperial office was established on 20 August 2026, with Reginald II becoming the first Emperor under the new constitutional arrangement.

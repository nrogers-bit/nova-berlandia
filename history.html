History of Nova-Berlandia

Explore the history of Nova-Berlandia, from the establishment of the First Commonwealth in 2025 to the present Imperial era.

A History of Nova-Berlandia

Nova-Berlandia has undergone several periods of constitutional, political and territorial change since its establishment in November 2025.

The modern Empire follows the earlier First Commonwealth, the Republic of Keystone period and the establishment of the Provisional Government in August 2026.

28 November 2025 – 17 February 2026

The First Commonwealth

On 28 November 2025, the United Commonwealth of Nova-Berlandia, also known as the First Commonwealth, was officially established.

The state was established as a transcontinental parliamentary semi-constitutional monarchy. The King of Nova-Berlandia served as head of state and held one third of the government's power, while the Prime Minister held another third and Parliament held the final third.

The First Prime Minister

For the first 11 days, Mateo Gomez-Villalta served as the first Prime Minister. He served as an independent throughout his term.

His administration was largely inactive, with Mateo attending approximately one to three meetings of Parliament.

The December 2025 Election

On 9 December 2025, the first general election of Nova-Berlandia was held to elect the Prime Minister.

Election Result Armani Ruiz received 45% of the voter percentage, followed by Mekhi Thomas with 35% and Muslima Abusamatova with 20%.

Armani Ruiz subsequently served as Prime Minister. His term was notably inactive, with King Shane von Berlandia handling bill proposals and parliamentary meetings for an extended period.

Vote of Confidence

On 5 January 2026, Shane von Berlandia issued a vote of confidence in Parliament to review Armani Ruiz's performance as Prime Minister.

Out of the 11 members of Parliament, five participated in the vote, including the King, who had presided over Parliament throughout the nation's history at that point.

Result Armani Ruiz was removed from office, with 60% of participating parliamentary voters voting to remove him.

The decision was criticized by Leader of the Opposition Muslima Abusamatova, who argued that the vote had been conducted contrary to the constitutional provisions governing votes of confidence.

The Mekhi Thomas Government

Following Armani Ruiz's removal, Mekhi Thomas was inaugurated as the third Prime Minister during the parliamentary session in which the vote of confidence took place.

During Mekhi Thomas's term, the Weather Bill was passed, establishing the Nova-Berlandian Weather Service. The service officially functioned for one day in preparation for a major winter storm and issued its first and only warning the following day.

On 30 January 2026, two bills were passed unanimously: the Veteran Basic Needs Guarantee Act and the Project and Creation Act.

The February 2026 Election

On 9 February 2026, the second general election was held. Mekhi Thomas chose not to seek another term as Prime Minister.

Progressive Equality instead put forward Kalisiana Grasty as its candidate.

Election Result Muslima Abusamatova won with seven votes, receiving 46.7% of the voter percentage. Madelyn Fidler received four votes, representing 26.7%.

The End of the First Commonwealth

Nova-Berlandia was declared inactive on 17 February 2026 and was replaced by the Republic of Keystone.

August 2026

The Provisional Government

On 20 August 2026, the Provisional Government of Nova-Berlandia was established, marking the beginning of the current period of Nova-Berlandian government.

The provisional government was headed jointly by Shane, serving as Emperor, and Jackson Hogan, serving as Prime Minister.

Its establishment followed a brief period of political and territorial uncertainty involving Nova-Berlandia and the neighboring Grand Principality of Berlandia.

The Territorial Dispute

During this period, a brief territorial dispute emerged between Nova-Berlandia and the Grand Principality of Berlandia concerning their respective territorial claims.

Although the dispute was short-lived, it resulted in a significant political settlement.

Political Settlement The Grand Principality of Berlandia was reformed into the Kingdom of Torvaley, while Nova-Berlandia secured its independence and received an additional province as part of the settlement.
September 2026

The Constitutional and Imperial Era

Constitutional Reform

On 30 August 2026, a draft Constitution was formally proposed to the House of Commons, the lower house of the Parliament of Nova-Berlandia.

The draft passed the House of Commons by a vote of 8–0 on the following day before proceeding to a national referendum on 1 September 2026.

Constitutional Referendum The Constitution passed by a landslide, with 16 citizens voting in favour and 1 citizen voting against.

Diplomatic Dispute with Morella

On 1 September 2026, a diplomatic dispute occurred between Nova-Berlandia and the French micronation and mock government of Morella over proposed legislative and executive reforms.

The dispute resulted in a national emergency being declared in Nova-Berlandia after Morella, in alliance with the Emu Empire and the micronational raider group Order of Nexizaria (NXI), came close to declaring war.

The Order of Nexizaria was the only group to make a move during the dispute, leading to a second national emergency being declared by Prime Minister Jackson Hogan.

The New Greenian Succession Claim

On 2 September 2026, Emperor Reginald II announced a dynastic succession claim to the New Greenian throne through the Sovereign Empire of New Greenia.

The claim was based on the period in Verdanian history during which Reginald I, his father, served as Emperor.

The succession claim was later rescinded.

Present Day

Nova-Berlandia Today

Nova-Berlandia currently operates under its modern constitutional system and the Imperial Crown.

The Empire continues to develop its government, institutions and national identity following the constitutional reforms of 2026.

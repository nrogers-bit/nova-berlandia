Government

Learn about the constitutional institutions and government of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

The Emperor

Emperor Reginald II

Emperor of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia

The Emperor of Nova-Berlandia is the constitutional head of state and serves as a symbol of national unity and security.

The office of Emperor was established in its present form on 20 August 2026, with Reginald II serving as the first Emperor of the present Imperial Crown.

Head of State Constitutional head of the Empire.
National Security Serves as a symbol of the nation's security.
Protector of the Constitution Protector and upholder of the Constitution.
Chief Diplomat Represents Nova-Berlandia in diplomatic affairs.
National Unity Serves as a person of national unity.
Parliament A lifelong sitting member of Parliament.

The Imperial Crown

The Imperial Crown of Nova-Berlandia

The Imperial Berlandian Crown is the institution of the Emperor and represents the constitutional monarchy of the Empire of Nova-Berlandia.

Under the Second Constitution of Nova-Berlandia, the Emperor is the person of national unity and a symbol of the nation's security as a whole.

House of Torres

The Imperial Family

The imperial family is officially known as the House of Torres, consisting of the family of the incumbent Emperor.
